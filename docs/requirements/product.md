# Product Requirements

## Vision

Help renters find trustworthy homes in Thailand and help legitimate agents manage listings without noisy manual coordination.

## Primary Users

- Renters searching for condos, apartments, houses, villas, and serviced apartments.
- Agents and owners publishing inventory.
- Admins verifying listings and moderating suspicious content.

## MVP Scope

- Browse listings with location, price, property type, bedrooms, and availability filters.
- View property details with images, map-ready coordinates, amenities, agent profile, and inquiry form.
- Agent accounts can create, edit, hide, confirm, and delete their own listings.
- Renters can favorite properties and submit inquiries.
- Admins can verify listings, feature listings, and hide listings.

## Out of Scope for MVP

- Payments, subscriptions, promoted listing billing, and contracts.
- Real-time chat.
- Automated property valuation.
- Multi-language content management beyond English-ready structure.

## Success Metrics

- A renter can find a relevant property in under three search refinements.
- An agent can publish a complete listing in under five minutes.
- Admins can review and verify listings without database access.
